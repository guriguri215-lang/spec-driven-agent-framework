# Execution Plans

Long-running or multi-part work uses a living execution plan under
`docs/exec-plans/active/`.

Every plan must include:

- Objective and measurable completion state.
- Scope and non-goals.
- Source requirements and acceptance criteria.
- Dependencies, risks, and assumptions.
- Checkpoints and validation commands.
- Stop conditions.
- Technical sandbox handling.
- Owner approval gates.
- Language and publication boundaries.
- Decision and progress logs.

Move completed plans to `docs/exec-plans/completed/` only after their evidence
and final Git state are recorded.
